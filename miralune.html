Case study

MIRALUNE

Skin, by moonlight.

Studio concept · self-initiated

Kind of shop
Small skincare label
What we made
Name, logo, four products, labels, gift box, film
Film
15s

Most skincare sells the morning: glow, protection, a fresh start. Miralune sells the ten minutes before sleep, when the phone is face down and nobody is watching.

(I) The brief

A small label with four products and one evening ritual: cleanse, tone, renew, seal. It has to look calm on a shelf, read clearly on a small label in a dim bathroom, and feel like a gift when the box is opened on camera.

(II) The idea

A crescent holding one drop.

Mira means to look and wonder; lune is the moon. The mark is a crescent cradling a single drop, the moment the serum lands. Night Ink, Dusk Clay and Petal Blush carry it, with one thin line of gold and nothing louder.

(III) In use

Four products, one ritual.

Each product gets its own colour from the palette, so the order of the ritual is visible from across the room. On every label, the product name is the biggest text, because that is what a tired customer is looking for.

More from the project

(IV) What it is built to do

It is a concept, so there are no sales figures to show. In a real job, these are the two things we would check with you after launch:

  1. 01The right bottle, in the dark: a customer finds the serum on a dim shelf without reading the back.
  2. 02The box gets filmed: buyers open the gift box on camera and post it without being asked.
