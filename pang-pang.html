Case study

PANG PANG

A sandwich you can hear from the street.

Studio concept · self-initiated

Kind of shop
Street-food cart and delivery
What we made
Name, logo, cart livery, wrap paper, posters, film
Film
12s

From ten metres away, most sandwich carts look the same: a menu board, a price, a queue. We wanted a cart people could pick out by its colour and its noise before they could read a word.

(I) The brief

One cart on a busy corner, plus a delivery app. The menu is short: four baguette sandwiches and a choice of how much chilli. The brand has two jobs. Be read in one second by someone walking past, and still work as a tiny icon on a phone.

(II) The idea

Loud on purpose.

Pang is the sandwich, and it is also the sound a fresh crust makes. So the brand is loud on purpose: a scored baguette with a chilli smirk, printed in two inks like a street poster, and a name repeated until it becomes a pattern.

(III) In use

Wrapped loud, ordered fast.

The same two inks carry the wrap paper, the seal sticker and the order screen. In the app, the hottest chilli level is called PANG PANG, so the joke and the menu are the same thing.

More from the project

(IV) What it is built to do

It is a concept, so there are no sales figures to show. In a real job, these are the two things we would check with you after launch:

  1. 01One look is enough: a passer-by can name the cart after seeing it once.
  2. 02People order by the brand’s words: regulars ask for “PANG PANG” at the counter.
