How it works

  1. 01Message on WhatsAppSend BRAND, WEB, FILM or AUDIT, and tell us what you sell and who buys it.
  2. 02A written planA short call, then a one-page plan with scope, timeline and quote. No surprise costs later.
  3. 03Two or three directionsConcepts shown on real things: your signboard, your bag, your phone screen. You pick one.
  4. 04Build and hand overFinal files, a simple brand guide and launch posts, ready to use on day one.

The operator.

Xavier Studio is run by Melvin Pang. Before the studio, he spent fifteen years in hotels, HR and operations: front desks, rosters and the WhatsApp chat at 11pm.

So the work starts from how a shop really runs, not only from how the logo looks. Every project is directed by him, with AI tools doing the heavy rendering. Ask, and he will tell you which is which.

Before the studio

  • Now: brands, websites and films for shops like those.
