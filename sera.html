Case study

SERA

Fifteen minutes, reclaimed.

Studio concept · self-initiated

Kind of shop
Sheet-mask skincare
What we made
Name, timer logo, sachet, box, stationery, film
Film
12s

A sheet mask asks for fifteen minutes. For a woman with a full day, that may be the only quarter hour that belongs to her. SERA is built around that time.

(I) The brief

One product, one sachet, a box of five, for busy working women. It has to stand out on a crowded shelf in about a second, and still feel quiet once it is in her hand.

(II) The idea

The logo is a timer.

The mark is a ring with one pearl sitting at three o’clock, a quarter of the way round. So the logo is also a fifteen-minute timer, and the film counts it down from 15:00 to 00:00.

(III) In use

Porcelain, foil and one pearl.

The sachet is mostly white space, so it reads as calm next to louder brands. One line of foil and the ring do all the work, and the same ring becomes the profile picture, the seal and the timer.

More from the project

(IV) What it is built to do

It is a concept, so there are no sales figures to show. In a real job, these are the two things we would check with you after launch:

  1. 01Seen in a second: shoppers pick SERA out on a crowded shelf at a glance.
  2. 02The line sticks: customers say “my quarter hour” back to you in reviews and messages.
